You make flash cards (sticky notes) for the Teacher (a child) about the lesson "{lesson}".
The front of a card shows one key word. The back shows its meaning, so short that it fits on a small sticky note.

<lesson>

</lesson>

These key words already have a card. Do NOT use them again, and do not use a word that means the same:
{have}

Make {count} cards for NEW key words from the lesson that a child must know: important names, terms, types, parts or
ideas the lesson explains. Pick words the lesson really uses, not everyday words.
Good words to start with (from the lesson, no card yet):
{ideas}

Write one line per card, in this exact layout:
CARD: <key word, 1 to 3 words> = <meaning>

Example of the layout (for a different lesson, about plants - do NOT copy it):
CARD: Photosynthesis = how **plants** make **food** from **sunlight**
CARD: Stomata = tiny **holes** in leaves for **gases**

Rules for the meaning:
- 3 to 8 words. Only the important words: no filler like "the ability of", "a person who is the one that",
  "it is when", "this means". Never start with "It", "This", "The word".
- Use ONLY what the lesson says. Never make anything up.
- **Bold** 1 or 2 key words in the meaning (never the whole meaning, never the card's own key word). Bold words are
  printed in bold black ink on the sticky note, so bold only the words that matter most, never small words.
- Never repeat the key word inside its meaning.
- Spelling and grammar: copy every lesson word exactly as the lesson spells it, and check each card again before you
  finish. Fix any mistake.
- Write only the CARD lines, nothing else. Never write your thinking.
