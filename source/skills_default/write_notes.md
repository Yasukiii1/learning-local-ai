Write simple, easy-to-follow study notes for the Teacher (a child) about your part of the lesson "{lesson}".
Your part is about: {topic}

<text>

</text>

Format (Markdown):
- Start with "## {topic}".
- Then short bullet points ("- "), one idea each, in simple words. Use "### " small headings if there are a few
  different ideas.
- **Bold** the important key words (1-3 words, like **photosynthesis**). Never bold a whole sentence.
- End with one line: "💡 Remember: " and the single most important idea.

Rules:
- Use ONLY what the text says. Nothing from outside the lesson. Never make up numbers, names or dates.
- Keep it short: at most about 12 bullet points.
- Write only the notes, no greeting, no other text.
