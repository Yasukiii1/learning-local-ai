The Teacher (a child) is reviewing a question they got wrong or ran out of time on.

The question: "{question}"
The correct answer: "{correct}"

Write ONE short, simple sentence that explains the main idea, like a clear definition a child can remember
(this works for any subject).

How to mark the key words:
- Put **two stars** around the 1 or 2 most important lesson words, like this: **word**.
- A key word is a short term (1-3 words) from the question or the answer, never a whole sentence.

Example (for a different lesson, about plants - do NOT copy it):
**Photosynthesis** is how a plant uses **sunlight** to make its own food.

Rules:
- ONE sentence, at most about 20 words, in simple words.
- Only facts from the question and the answer. Nothing from outside the lesson.
- No opinion about the Teacher's answer (no "good try", "don't worry").
- Write ONLY the sentence: no greeting, no label, no notes about these instructions.
- Spelling and grammar: check every word is spelled correctly (copy lesson words exactly as the text spells them)
  and every sentence is complete and makes sense. Fix any mistake before you finish.
