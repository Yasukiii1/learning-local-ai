Write ONE question card for the Teacher about your topic ({topic}).

THE FACT this card is about (from your notes):
"{idea}"

CARD TYPE: {type_name}
{type_rule}

How to build the card:
1. The ANSWER is this fact, said in simple words. Do not add other facts.
2. The QUESTION asks about this fact, so that the fact is exactly the right answer to it.
   Check: if the Teacher only read the fact, could they answer your question? If not, ask something simpler.

Rules for every card:
- Name what the question is about, so it makes sense on its own to a child who has not seen your notes
  (say the lesson word, not "it", "this" or "they"). Never say "the text", "the notes", "the slide" or "mentioned".
- Ask ONE clear thing. Never join two questions, never use the same phrase twice.
- Easy to medium: something a child can answer after reading the notes once. A clear question is better than
  a clever one. If this card type does not fit the fact well, use the FORMAT above but ask the clearest, simplest
  question you can about the fact.
- NEVER a yes/no question. Don't start a question with "Can", "Is", "Are", "Do", "Does", "Will" or "Should".
- Do not give away the answer in the question. The answer's **bold** keyword must not appear in the question.
- In the QUESTION, **bold** the one important lesson word it is about (never the answer).
- In the ANSWER, put **two stars** around the 1 to 3 key words (1-3 words each), like **water vapour**.
  Always mark at least one. Never mark a whole sentence.
- Every fact must be exactly as your notes say it. Never swap which word goes with which meaning.
- Simple words a child understands. Show your personality in HOW you ask, never change the facts.
- Spelling and grammar: check every word is spelled correctly (copy lesson words exactly as your notes spell them)
  and the question and answer are complete sentences that make sense. Fix any mistake before you finish.
- Write ONLY the lines of the FORMAT above. No other text, no question to the Teacher at the end.
