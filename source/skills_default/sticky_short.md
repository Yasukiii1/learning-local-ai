These flash-card meanings are too long for a small sticky note. Make each one SHORT: 3 to 7 words.

{cards}

How to shorten: keep only the key words, drop the rest. A list keeps its 2 most important items.

Examples of shortening (for other lessons - do NOT copy them):
LONG: Photosynthesis = the process that green plants use in order to make their own food by using the light of the sun
SHORT: Photosynthesis = plants make **food** from **sunlight**
LONG: Herbivore = an animal that only eats plants such as grass, leaves, fruits, seeds and other plant parts
SHORT: Herbivore = animal that eats only **plants**
LONG: Democracy = a system of government in which the people choose their leaders by voting in elections (e.g. a president)
SHORT: Democracy = people **vote** to choose **leaders**

Rules:
- 3 to 7 words. Never more than 7.
- Keep the facts exactly as they are. Never add anything new and never change the meaning.
- **Bold** 1 or 2 key words in the meaning (never the card's own key word). Bold words are printed in bold black ink on the
  sticky note, so bold only the words that matter most (a name, a number, the main idea), never small words.
- Spell every word correctly.

Write one line per card, in the same order, in this exact layout:
SHORT: <key word> = <short meaning>

Write only the SHORT lines, nothing else.
